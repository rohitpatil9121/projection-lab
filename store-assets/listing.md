## Full description (4000 CHARACTER limit — not words)

> Play's limit is 4000 **characters**. The draft below is ~2,900, leaving room to add
> keywords later without a rewrite.

```
Financial Blueprint turns your real numbers — salary, SIPs, EPF, PPF, NPS, loans and property — into a year-by-year picture of your financial future. No jargon, no generic advice, and everything in ₹ lakh and crore rather than millions.

WHAT IT DOES

• Net-worth projection, year by year
See your assets, liabilities and net worth from today to age 100. Every figure comes from inputs you control, so you can always trace where a number came from.

• Plan with live sliders
Drag retirement age, monthly SIP, expected return and retirement spending, and watch the whole projection redraw instantly. Save what you like as your plan, or reset back to where you started.

• Goals you save for AND spend
A goal isn't only a target — some cost money on the day they arrive. Set a goal to save toward an amount, to save and then spend it at a chosen age (a car, a wedding, education), or simply to mark a date on your timeline. Each goal shows progress, the SIP required to get there, and whether you're ahead or behind.

• Monte Carlo risk analysis
Markets don't return the same number every year. Run 250, 500 or 1000 randomized simulations and see your success probability alongside best-case, median and worst-case outcomes.

• Accounts and loans that behave properly
Track savings, equity, mutual funds, EPF, PPF, NPS, property and loans. Link an EMI to the loan it services and the projection actually retires that debt instead of just spending the money.

• Cash flow at a glance
Income versus outflow for the month, your savings rate, and how much of it is reaching your investments.

• What-if scenarios
Compare alternative plans side by side — retire earlier, invest more, buy a house — without touching your main plan.

• Import your mutual fund CAS
Bring in your holdings from a CAS statement instead of typing them one by one.

BUILT FOR INDIA

Amounts in lakh and crore. EPF, PPF, NPS and SIP as first-class concepts. Old and new tax regime assumptions. Inflation-adjusted targets so a goal set today reflects what it will actually cost then.

YOUR DATA

Your plan is stored on your device. Sign in only if you want it backed up and synced — with email and password, or with Google. You can delete your account and everything on our servers at any time from Settings. We do not sell your data, and there are no ads or third-party trackers.

TRY IT WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT

Open the app and tap "Go with sample data" to explore a fully populated plan — from a fresh graduate to a high-net-worth portfolio — before entering anything of your own.

IMPORTANT

Financial Blueprint is a planning and illustration tool. It is not investment advice, and we are not a registered investment adviser. Projections are estimates based on the assumptions you enter; real returns will differ. Please consult a qualified adviser before making financial decisions.
```

| Type | Collected | Shared | Required | Purpose |
